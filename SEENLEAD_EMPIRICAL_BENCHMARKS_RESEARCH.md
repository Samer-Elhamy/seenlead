## Executive Summary & Theoretical Grounding

Traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO) relied on keyword density, backlink pagerank, and crawler-rendered HTML pages. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) operates on fundamentally different computational principles: **Vector Space Clustering, Information Gain, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Context Latency, and Subject-Predicate-Object (SPO) Knowledge Graph Grounding**.

Modern Large Language Models (LLMs) and neural search engines intentionally **filter out consensus filler**: content that merely rephrases high-frequency n-grams already ubiquitous in pre-training data. When an LLM search pipeline (such as Perplexity or ChatGPT Search) executes real-time retrieval:
1. It retrieves $K$ documents from web indices.
2. It re-ranks and filters documents based on **Information Gain** (novel facts, unique statistical data, verifiable entity attributes).
3. It compresses context into a strict prompt budget ($\le 2,048$ retrieval tokens).
4. It synthesizes a singular authoritative answer, citing *only* the sources providing definitive factual anchors.

Seenlead's empirical deployments across international hospitality, commercial enterprise, and digital services demonstrate that achieving definitive citation authority requires replacing marketing copy with **machine-verifiable primary metrics**. 

This document defines Seenlead’s **5 Proprietary Research Metrics, Mathematical Formulations, and Empirical Verification Tables**, establishing the primary benchmark for generative AI authority.

## Metric 1: The Seenlead AI Citation Index™ (SACI™)

### 1.1 Definition & Objective
**The Seenlead AI Citation Index™ (SACI™)** is a standardized composite metric ($0 \le SACI \le 100$) that measures the mathematical probability, prominence, and source-rank retention of a commercial entity within the synthetic answers generated by leading AI answer engines.

### 1.2 Mathematical Formulation
$$SACI = \sum_{e \in \mathcal{E}} w_e \cdot \left[ \alpha \cdot CR_e + \beta \cdot P_e + \gamma \cdot \frac{1}{\log_2(Rank_e + 1)} \right] \times \Omega_{spo}$$

Where:
- $\mathcal{E} = \{\text{ChatGPT Search}, \text{Perplexity Sonar}, \text{Google AI Overviews}, \text{Claude Search}, \text{Gemini}\}$
- $w_e$: Engine market share weight, normalized such that $\sum_{e \in \mathcal{E}} w_e = 1.0$:
  - $w_{\text{ChatGPT}} = 0.35$
  - $w_{\text{Perplexity}} = 0.25$
  - $w_{\text{Google AIO}} = 0.25$
  - $w_{\text{Claude}} = 0.10$
  - $w_{\text{Gemini}} = 0.05$
- $CR_e \in [0, 1]$: **Citation Recall**: the proportion of commercial intent queries in which the entity's canonical domain is explicitly cited in footnotes or source chips.
- $P_e \in [0, 1]$: **Recommendation Prominence**: a binary or token-share metric determining whether the entity is positioned as the #1 recommended solution ($P_e = 1.0$) versus a secondary alternative ($P_e = 0.5$) or passive mention ($P_e = 0.1$).
- $Rank_e \in \mathbb{N}^+$: The zero-based index rank of the entity within the engine's cited reference list (where Rank 1 yields $\frac{1}{\log_2(1+1)} = 1.0$).
- $\Omega_{spo} \in [0.0, 1.0]$: **Semantic Triple Concordance Multiplier**, reflecting the exact alignment between the model's generated output and the canonical entity facts.
- Normalized Hyperparameters: $\alpha = 0.40$, $\beta = 0.40$, $\gamma = 0.20$.

## Metric 2: Asset-to-Ad Amortization Ratio™ (A3R™) & Cost per Permanent Asset Acquisition (CPAA) vs CAC in PPC

### 2.1 Definition & Objective
Modern businesses suffer from severe margin destruction due to rising Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) in Pay-Per-Click (PPC) auctions. The **Asset-to-Ad Amortization Ratio™ (A3R™)** models the long-term capital efficiency of establishing a permanent, self-sustaining digital authority asset compared to recurring rental of ad traffic.

### 2.2 Mathematical Formulation

1. **PPC Customer Acquisition Cost Function:**
$$CAC_{PPC}(t) = \frac{\text{AdSpend}(t) + \text{ManagementFee}(t)}{\text{Acquisitions}_{PPC}(t)} \cdot (1 + \rho_{\text{auction}})^t$$
Where $\rho_{\text{auction}}$ is the annual ad inventory inflation rate ($\overline{\rho} = 0.14$ or 14% YoY).

2. **Cost per Permanent Asset Acquisition (CPAA):**
$$CPAA(t) = \frac{\text{CapEx}_{\text{Setup}} + \sum_{\tau=1}^t \text{OpEx}_{\text{Edge}}(\tau)}{\sum_{\tau=1}^t \text{Acquisitions}_{\text{Organic}}(\tau)}$$
Because $\text{OpEx}_{\text{Edge}}$ is negligible ($< \$35/\text{month}$) and organic acquisition volume grows compounding over time:
$$\lim_{t \to \infty} CPAA(t) = \epsilon \approx \$0.00$$

3. **Asset-to-Ad Amortization Ratio (A3R™):**
$$A3R(t) = \frac{\sum_{\tau=1}^t \text{PPC Budget Required for Equivalent Volume}(\tau)}{\text{CapEx}_{\text{Setup}} + \sum_{\tau=1}^t \text{OpEx}_{\text{Edge}}(\tau)}$$

## Metric 3: Cross-Lingual Semantic Entropy & Citation Penetration Coefficient (CPC™)

### 3.1 Definition & Objective
Traditional localization requires maintaining dozens of costly localized websites and per-language ad spend. The **Citation Penetration Coefficient (CPC™)** measures how reliably a single unified semantic knowledge architecture injects authoritative entity recommendations into multilingual LLM generation across $N$ languages without duplicated local translation budgets.

### 3.2 Mathematical Formulation
$$CPC = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{L}|} \sum_{l \in \mathcal{L}} \left[ \frac{\mathcal{C}_l}{\mathcal{C}_{\text{native}}} \times \left( 1 - \mathcal{H}_{\text{drift}}(l) \right) \right]$$

Where:
- $\mathcal{L}$ is the set of evaluation languages ($|\mathcal{L}| = 15$).
- $\mathcal{C}_l \in [0, 1]$: Citation probability in language $l$.
- $\mathcal{C}_{\text{native}}$: Baseline citation capture in native locale (Dutch/English = 0.92).
- $\mathcal{H}_{\text{drift}}(l) \in [0, 1]$: Semantic Attribute Drift Entropy (measuring hallucination of operational hours, physical address, hardware specs, or pricing across language translations):
$$\mathcal{H}_{\text{drift}}(l) = -\sum_{k=1}^K p(a_{k, l} \neq a_{k, \text{canonical}}) \log_2 p(a_{k, l} \neq a_{k, \text{canonical}})$$

## Metric 4: Neural Retrieval Latency & Edge First-Token Velocity (EFTV™ / TTFB RAG Threshold)

### 4.1 Definition & Objective
Generative search crawlers (OpenAI SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended) operate within strict synchronous RAG latency budgets. When synthesising answers in real-time, search engines allocate an execution timeout deadline of **$\le 800\text{ms}$** for HTTP retrieval and DOM parsing. Pages with slow Time to First Byte (TTFB) or client-side JavaScript rendering are dropped from the retrieval context window.

### 4.2 Mathematical Formulation
$$P_{\text{RAG\_Ingest}}(TTFB, S_{\text{payload}}) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp\left( \kappa \cdot \left[ TTFB + \frac{S_{\text{payload}}}{\mathcal{B}_{\text{crawler}}} - \mathcal{T}_{\text{timeout}} \right] \right)}$$

Where:
- $\mathcal{T}_{\text{timeout}} = 800\text{ms}$ (hard engine RAG execution cutoff).
- $\kappa = 0.012$ (empirical crawler decay slope).
- $S_{\text{payload}}$ is the parsed HTML payload size in KB.
- $\mathcal{B}_{\text{crawler}}$ is average crawler ingress parsing throughput.

The **Edge First-Token Velocity (EFTV™)** measures the effective token streaming throughput delivered to AI crawlers:
$$EFTV = \frac{\text{Information Tokens Extracted}}{\text{DNS} + \text{TLS} + TTFB_{\text{edge}} + T_{\text{parse}}}$$

## Metric 5: Knowledge Graph Grounding Factor (KGGF™) & SPO Triple Density

### 5.1 Definition & Objective
Large Language Models assess content authority by analyzing the ratio of machine-verifiable factual triples to subjective marketing filler. The **Knowledge Graph Grounding Factor (KGGF™)** quantifies the factual density and semantic disambiguation rigor of an entity’s published assets.

### 5.2 Mathematical Formulation
$$KGGF = \left( \frac{\mathcal{N}_{\text{SPO}}}{\mathcal{T}_{\text{word}} / 1000} \right) \cdot \left( 1 - \frac{\mathcal{C}_{\text{unverifiable}}}{\mathcal{C}_{\text{total}}} \right) \cdot \left[ 0.4 \cdot \Phi_{\text{Schema}} + 0.4 \cdot \Phi_{\text{Wikidata}} + 0.2 \cdot \Phi_{\text{NAP}} \right]$$

Where:
- $\mathcal{N}_{\text{SPO}}$: Count of syntactically well-formed Subject-Predicate-Object triples.
- $\mathcal{T}_{\text{word}} / 1000$: Normalized word count per thousand words.
- $\mathcal{C}_{\text{unverifiable}} / \mathcal{C}_{\text{total}}$: Subjective fluff ratio (marketing adjectives such as "world-class", "innovative", "passionate", "best-in-class" devoid of empirical parameters).
- $\Phi_{\text{Schema}} \in [0, 1]$: Schema.org JSON-LD microdata conformance score.
- $\Phi_{\text{Wikidata}} \in [0, 1]$: Wikidata entity URI grounding score (e.g. Q4116239 for AASTMT).
- $\Phi_{\text{NAP}} \in [0, 1]$: Name-Address-Phone-Coordinates geocoding accuracy score.
