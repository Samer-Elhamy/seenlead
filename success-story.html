Success story

Shisha Lounge Angelo — Amsterdam

Our one public case study. We don’t invent a project grid — here’s the full story, then the industries we serve.

Before

Angelo’s lounge competed for attention with paid ads and word of mouth. Travelers asking in other languages often never heard his name. Visibility depended on keep spending.

What we did

We mapped the questions guests actually ask, strengthened his digital footprint, and managed the presence that search and AI use to recommend a place — including the signals that help Google show a local business clearly.

After

Angelo shows up as the first name across many languages. Guests find him without a monthly ad meter. Reviews stay strong. Sales moved up while ad dependence went down.
